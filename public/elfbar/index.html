INTRODUCING ELFBAR

Design-led from the beginning.

ELFBAR’s story began in 2018 with a clear ambition: to rethink the everyday vaping experience through considered product design, distinctive flavour development and increasingly refined technology.

Its focus on intuitive devices has grown into a globally recognised vaping brand, with international design and industry awards. From the way a device sits in the hand to how it manages power, e-liquid and vapour production, the details shape the experience.
