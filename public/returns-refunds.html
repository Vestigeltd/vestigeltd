Vestige Ltd Returns & Refunds Policy

At Vestige, every ELFBAR BC10000 is supplied as a new, sealed, single-use product. Because the product contains an e-liquid reservoir and is intended for personal consumption, we take returns, product integrity and hygiene extremely seriously.

Change-of-Mind Returns

For qualifying electronic transactions, the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002 (ECTA) may provide a seven-day cooling-off period calculated from the date the consumer receives the goods.

Where an applicable change-of-mind return is permitted, the direct cost of returning the product is payable by the consumer. The ELFBAR BC10000 must remain completely unopened, unused and in its original condition.

Sealed & Unused Requirement

For a change-of-mind return, the ELFBAR BC10000 must be:

  • Unused and unactivated;
  • Completely unopened;
  • In its original manufacturer packaging;
  • Complete with all supplied packaging, labels and components; and
  • Free from leakage, damage, tampering or modification.

A disposable vape that has been opened or used cannot be returned as a change-of-mind return.

This does not affect any statutory right to return a product that is defective, unsafe, incorrectly supplied or otherwise does not meet applicable South African consumer-law requirements.

Damaged on Delivery

Please inspect your parcel upon delivery. If your ELFBAR BC10000 arrives damaged, crushed, leaking, broken or otherwise compromised, do not use or activate it.

Contact Vestige promptly and provide photographs of:

  1. The outer shipping packaging;
  2. The ELFBAR packaging;
  3. The product; and
  4. The delivery label/order information.

Vestige may require the product to be returned for inspection. Where a transit-damage claim is validated, Vestige will provide the appropriate remedy in accordance with applicable South African consumer law.

Defective Product

If an ELFBAR BC10000 is defective or does not function as reasonably expected, contact Vestige with details of the fault.

Where applicable, statutory consumer rights and remedies under South African law will apply, including the implied warranty of quality provided by the Consumer Protection Act.

Proof of Purchase

A valid proof of purchase may include your:

  • Vestige order confirmation;
  • Invoice or receipt; or
  • Order number or other reasonable evidence confirming the purchase from Vestige.

Vestige may be unable to process a voluntary return where the purchase cannot be verified.

How to Request a Return

Do not send a product back without receiving return instructions from Vestige first.

Contact customer support and provide:

  • Order number;
  • Customer name;
  • Date received;
  • Product/flavour;
  • Reason for return;
  • Description of the issue; and
  • Photographs/video, where relevant.

This allows Vestige to verify your order, assess the request and provide the correct return procedure.

Returns We Cannot Accept

Vestige cannot accept a change-of-mind return where the product has been used, activated, consumed, opened, refilled, recharged, dismantled, modified, repaired, damaged after delivery or otherwise altered by the customer.

This restriction does not apply where applicable South African law provides a statutory right or remedy relating to a defective, unsafe or incorrectly supplied product.

Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude, restrict or waive any consumer right that cannot lawfully be excluded under South African law.

Where this policy conflicts with a mandatory provision of applicable law, the law will prevail.
