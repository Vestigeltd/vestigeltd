ELFBAR BC10000

High capacity without the ceremony.

The BC10000 brings ELFBAR’s approach into a compact, rechargeable single-use device—combining a pre-filled e-liquid system, rechargeable 620 mAh battery, mesh coil and integrated real-time display within an 85 × 43 × 22 mm form factor.

Inhale to activate, monitor the device, recharge when necessary and replace the unit when the e-liquid is exhausted. No refill routine. No firing button. No coil change.

COMPONENT STORY

Coordinated, not complicated.

A draw activates the heating system, the mesh coil vaporises the pre-filled e-liquid, the rechargeable battery supplies power and the display communicates remaining status. The result is a direct operating experience with minimal setup or routine maintenance.

Quality starts with authenticity. ELFBAR advises checking the package QR code on elfbar.com and looking for its holographic security thread. Product appearance alone cannot prove authenticity.
