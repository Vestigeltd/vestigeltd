Vestige Privacy Policy

Vestige Ltd respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website or place an order, we may collect:

  • Name and contact details;
  • Delivery and billing information;
  • Order and transaction details;
  • Communications with Vestige; and
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser and device information.

We collect only information reasonably necessary for legitimate business purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Process and fulfil orders;
  • Arrange payments and delivery;
  • Communicate with you and provide customer support;
  • Improve our website, products and services;
  • Prevent fraud, misuse and unlawful activity; and
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We process personal information only where permitted by applicable law.

We will not sell or rent your personal information.

3. Sharing Your Information

We may share relevant information with trusted service providers where reasonably necessary to operate our business, including payment processors, delivery providers, technology providers and professional advisers.

Information will only be shared where lawful and reasonably necessary.

4. Marketing

We may send promotional communications where permitted by law. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time.

5. Cookies & Website Data

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to maintain functionality, improve performance and understand website usage. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

6. Security & Retention

Vestige takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure or alteration.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary or as required by law.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

  • Access personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated information;
  • Request deletion where legally permitted;
  • Object to certain processing; or
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

8. Contact & Complaints

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact Vestige using the contact details published on our website.

If you believe your personal information has been handled unlawfully, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

9. Changes to This Policy

Vestige may update this Privacy Policy where necessary to reflect changes to our business, technology or applicable law. The latest version published on the website will apply.
